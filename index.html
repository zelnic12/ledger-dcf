    Data comes from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints and is pulled live each time you look up a company. Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. The model grows FCF at your stage-one rate for five years, fades to terminal growth over years six to ten, then capitalises year ten with the Gordon growth formula. Banks, insurers and companies with large finance arms do not suit an FCF model. This is a calculator, not investment advice.
